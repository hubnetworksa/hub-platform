### Suggested new attractions
- **National Zoological Gardens (Pretoria Zoo)** (Family, Boom Street, Central Pretoria, R110, half a day (4-6 hours)): South Africa's national zoo, an 85-hectare site with exhibits covering mammals, birds, reptiles and an aquarium. Sources: https://www.pretoriazoo.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/cashless-nzg.pdf (official standard adult ticket price, valid from 1 January 2026), https://www.quickread.co.za/pretoria-zoo-everything-you-need-to-know-about-pretoria-zoo-south-africa/ (visit duration)
- **Ditsong National Museum of Natural History** (Heritage, 432 Paul Kruger Street, Central Pretoria, R60, 2-3 hours): South Africa's oldest natural history museum, with paleontology, zoology and geology collections housed in a historic building opposite the old City Hall. Sources: https://ditsong.org.za/en/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Natural-History-Museum-Admission-Fees.pdf (official adult self-guided tour fee, valid 1 April 2026 - 31 March 2027), https://www.kupi.com/en-ae/explore/south-africa/pretoria/ditsong-national-museum-of-natural-history (visit duration)
- **Pretoria National Botanical Garden** (Outdoors, Brummeria, R100 peak season (September-March; R80 off-peak April-August), 1.5-3 hours): A 76-hectare SANBI garden of indigenous South African flora with marked walking trails, on Cussonia Avenue in eastern Pretoria. Sources: https://www.sanbi.org/gardens/pretoria/visitor-information/information/ (official current entrance fees), https://www.gauteng.net/attractions/pretoria-national-botanical-garden-2/ (visit duration)
