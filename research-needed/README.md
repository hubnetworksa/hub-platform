# research-needed

One file per content-upgrade chunk that has businesses we couldn't verify ourselves. Fill in whatever you find, at your own pace — you don't need to finish a whole file before telling me about it, and you don't need to wait for me between chunks. The agents keep working through later chunks in the background regardless.

## Workflow

1. A chunk finishes -> a file appears here: `<city>/chunk-<NNN>.md`.
2. Each business in the file has its known facts (name, category, suburb, address, current description) and why it couldn't be verified, followed by a blank section for your research.
3. Fill in whatever you find under a business's "Your research" section, in this format:

   ```
   What it does / sells / specialises in:
   ...

   Who it serves:
   ...

   Anything notable:
   ...

   Recommendation: PUBLISH / HOLD / CLOSED
   ```

4. Leave a business untouched if you have nothing on it — it just stays deferred (its current text is kept, unchanged) and I won't ask about it again unless you come back to it later.
5. Tell me which file(s) you've worked on (even partially) whenever it suits you. I'll read the file, apply what's there, and move the file to `<city>/done/` once every business in it has been either applied or left deferred for good.

## Why this exists

Pasting research into chat for each chunk was slowing things down and bloating the conversation. Editing these files directly means you can work through them at your own speed, in your own editor, and the chunk pipeline doesn't have to pause and wait for you.
