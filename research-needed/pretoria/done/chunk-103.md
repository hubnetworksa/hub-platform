What it does / sells / specialises in:
CARRS stands for Corrective Action Request and Report System, a CSIR-developed ICT-based incident-management and workflow system for water-service delivery. It lets communities report water-related service and infrastructure incidents (leaks, bursts, supply interruptions), which are then routed to municipal officials for assessment, corrective action and tracking through to resolution. It also includes smart water-metering and USSD-based monitoring functionality.

Who it serves:
Water-service authorities, municipalities and the communities they supply.

Anything notable:
Developed by the CSIR through its water and wastewater infrastructure research work, and piloted in municipalities across South Africa, aimed at improving reporting, monitoring and resolution of municipal water-service incidents. The login portal at carrs.csir.co.za is the operational access point for the system rather than a marketing site, which is why it shows only a sign-in page to the public.
